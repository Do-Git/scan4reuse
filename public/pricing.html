SOME USEFUL DETAILS

Before the transaction.
Understand the fees.

Where in Switzerland does Scan4Reuse work?

Scan4Reuse works across the whole of Switzerland, connecting material owners, buyers and logistics partners. Basel → Zürich is a labelled example, and the delivery planner accepts your own Swiss pickup and destination.

How are the two 8% fees calculated?

The item-sale fee is 8% of the actual sold item value, deducted from the sale proceeds. The logistics fee is 8% of the partner’s final agreed delivery price after accepted adjustments, deducted from the partner’s amount. Each calculation uses its own transaction value.

What does the professional report contain?

The sample report lists identified materials, quantities, indicative resale values and estimated CO₂ savings. It is marked SAMPLE DATA. Final report scope and the methodology behind estimates must be confirmed for a live service.

Is there a monthly subscription?

There is no monthly subscription. Scan4Reuse deducts 8% from the completed item sale and a separate 8% from the logistics partner’s final agreed delivery price. Professional reports cost CHF 5 per square metre.

What sets the delivery price?

The logistics partner prices the route, dimensions, weight, crew, equipment and site access anywhere in Switzerland. The client reviews any adjusted scope and price. Our delivery fee is 8% of that final agreed price, covering transport, handling and added services, and is deducted from the partner’s amount.

Who pays the logistics transaction fee?

The logistics partner pays the delivery fee. For a final agreed delivery price of CHF 1,000, Scan4Reuse deducts CHF 80 and the partner receives CHF 920 before their own costs and taxes. If the accepted price changes to CHF 800, the fee becomes CHF 64 and the partner amount CHF 736. The separate item-sale fee still uses the sold item value.

How does a delivery quote work?

Specify collection and delivery requirements for locations across Switzerland. The partner accepts the request for review, quotes as requested or proposes adjusted scope and pricing, and returns it to the client for acceptance or revision. The 8% delivery fee follows the final agreed price.

How can I try the delivery workflow?

Open the client view and enter an item and Swiss route, or load the labelled Basel-to-Zürich example. Download a PDF brief, switch to the partner view to prepare or adjust a quote, then return to the client to review it. Both roles are an interactive example; no real shipment is booked.

Is the iPhone scanning app available?

The scanning screens illustrate a planned workflow for reusable materials. Enter confirmed measurements or use the window example. You can add existing photos and scan files in the delivery-documents workspace.

Are the sustainability figures verified?

The sample report uses estimated carbon savings and resale values. Our ESG content describes commitments and pilot goals, not verified project outcomes or certifications. Any future impact estimates need clear sources and assumptions.

About this prototype.

Scan4Reuse connects reuse sales and logistics across Switzerland. The client customizes collection, access and handling; a logistics partner quotes or proposes changes. The client can request a revision or accept the example proposal, with PDF downloads for both documents. Basel → Zürich is one illustrative route. Scanning and materials reports are supporting concepts.

Delivery requests are saved on this instance’s server, where its operator can access them. Partner proposals and example agreements stay in this browser session. No request is automatically sent to a carrier, no shipment is booked and no payment is taken. Please use sample details when trying the example.

The upload workspace is shared and has no account system. Do not upload private or sensitive files. No analytics or third-party tracking is used.
