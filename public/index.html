Why specify site access before quoting?

Every site has different access needs: a loading area, a collection contact, a reserved lift, suitable clearances or a final room. These details influence the crew, equipment and scope of the quote.

Recording those requirements early gives the client and logistics partner the same starting point. If the plan needs a different slot or service, the partner proposes it and the client decides.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you plan.
The useful details.

Who can use Scan4Reuse?

Material owners, buyers, architects, facility managers and logistics partners across Switzerland. Clients specify the item and delivery requirements; partners review them and propose a service and price.

How do the two 8% fees work?

The item-sale fee is 8% of the sold item value, deducted from the sale proceeds. The separate delivery fee is 8% of the logistics partner’s final agreed delivery price, including accepted adjustments, deducted from the partner’s amount. Each fee uses its own transaction value.

Can the logistics partner adjust the delivery?

Yes. A partner can propose different timing, handling, equipment, service or pricing. The client reviews the changes and can accept or request a revision. The delivery fee updates with the final agreed price.

What documents can I download?

Download a delivery brief with the item, route, access and handling requirements, and a partner-proposal PDF with the scope, price, fee breakdown and accepted adjustments. The current preview lets you try both roles; an example agreement does not book a shipment.

About this prototype.

Scan4Reuse connects reuse sales and logistics across Switzerland. The client customizes collection, access and handling; a logistics partner quotes or proposes changes. The client can request a revision or accept the example proposal, with PDF downloads for both documents. Basel → Zürich is one illustrative route. Scanning and materials reports are supporting concepts.

Delivery requests are saved on this instance’s server, where its operator can access them. Partner proposals and example agreements stay in this browser session. No request is automatically sent to a carrier, no shipment is booked and no payment is taken. Please use sample details when trying the example.

The upload workspace is shared and has no account system. Do not upload private or sensitive files. No analytics or third-party tracking is used.
