BORN AT A HACKATHON.
BUILT FOR REUSE.

We saw the manual effort behind identifying reusable building components — and the gap between finding a resource and getting it to its next site. Scan4Reuse connects the scan, the report, the marketplace and the journey.

About this prototype.

This website presents Scan4Reuse’s building-material reuse and logistics concept, using the branding, mockups and pricing model from the supplied founding deck. Team names and roles were confirmed by the team. Product screenshots, listings and report figures are samples, not live transactions or verified project results.

Delivery briefs are saved on this instance’s server. The operator of the instance can access them. No request is automatically sent to a carrier, no shipment is booked and no payment is taken. Please use sample details when trying the demo.

The upload workspace is shared and has no account system. Do not upload private or sensitive files. No analytics or third-party tracking is used.
