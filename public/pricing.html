SOME USEFUL DETAILS

Before the Basel pickup.
Agree the details.

What does the professional report contain?

The sample report lists identified materials, quantities, indicative resale values and estimated CO₂ savings. It is marked SAMPLE DATA. Final report scope and the methodology behind estimates must be confirmed for a live service.

Is there a monthly subscription?

There is no monthly subscription. Logistics partners pay 8% of each completed delivery transaction. The professional report costs CHF 5 per square metre, and the marketplace charges 8% of the item sale value.

What sets the Basel → Zürich delivery price?

The logistics partner prices the route, dimensions, weight, crew, equipment and site access. The proposal separates transport, handling and added services and states the VAT basis. The logistics partner pays Scan4Reuse 8% of the agreed delivery total. That fee comes from the partner’s amount and is not added on top of the client quote. The separate marketplace commission applies to the item sale value.

Who pays the logistics transaction fee?

The logistics partner pays Scan4Reuse 8% of the agreed delivery value for each completed transaction, covering transport, handling and added services. On an illustrative CHF 1,000 delivery, the fee is CHF 80 and the partner amount is CHF 920 before the partner’s own costs and taxes. No additional 8% is added to the client’s quote. Viewing a request or preparing a quote does not create a transaction fee.

How does a delivery quote work?

Start with the Basel window and specify collection and delivery requirements for Zürich. The partner accepts the request for review, quotes as requested or proposes changes, and returns it to the client for acceptance or revision.

How can I try the delivery workflow?

Open the client view, customize the Basel-to-Zürich request and download its PDF brief. Switch to the partner view to prepare a quote, then return to the client to review it. Both roles are an interactive example; no real shipment is booked.

Is the iPhone scanning app available?

The scanning screens illustrate a planned workflow. This example provides a window’s measurements for you to confirm or edit. You can add existing photos and scan files in the delivery-documents workspace.

Are the sustainability figures verified?

The sample report uses estimated carbon savings and resale values. Our ESG content describes commitments and pilot goals, not verified project outcomes or certifications. Any future impact estimates need clear sources and assumptions.

About this prototype.

This site follows an example delivery of a reclaimed window from Basel to Zürich. The client customizes collection, access and handling. A logistics partner reviews the request and prepares a quote or proposed changes. The client can request a revision or accept the example proposal, and both documents can be downloaded as PDFs. Scanning and materials reports are supporting concepts.

Delivery requests are saved on this instance’s server, where its operator can access them. Partner proposals and example agreements stay in this browser session. No request is automatically sent to a carrier, no shipment is booked and no payment is taken. Please use sample details when trying the example.

The upload workspace is shared and has no account system. Do not upload private or sensitive files. No analytics or third-party tracking is used.
