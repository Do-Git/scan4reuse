SOME USEFUL DETAILS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What does the professional report contain?

The sample report lists identified materials, quantities, indicative resale values and estimated CO₂ savings. It is marked SAMPLE DATA. Final report scope and the methodology behind estimates must be confirmed for a live service.

Is there a monthly subscription?

The pricing model uses two rates: CHF 5 per square metre for the professional report and an 8% marketplace commission on the total sold. There is no monthly subscription in this model.

Does the commission include transport?

Transport is quoted separately from the marketplace commission. A logistics partner needs the route, dimensions, weight, access and handling requirements to prepare a quote.

How does a delivery quote work?

You customize the good, route, timing, access and on-site services. A logistics partner accepts the request for review, then quotes as requested or proposes changes. You can accept the example proposal or ask for a revision.

Can I book a shipment or buy materials here?

You can explore the client and partner quote workflow and download the resulting documents. The example does not reserve materials, contact real partners, process sales, take payment or book a shipment.

Is the iPhone scanning app available?

The screenshots illustrate prototype concepts for the native scanning app and marketplace. You can explore the sample workflow here or upload scan files in the existing workspace.

Are the sustainability figures verified?

The sample report uses estimated carbon savings and resale values. Our ESG content describes commitments and pilot goals, not verified project outcomes or certifications. Any future impact estimates need clear sources and assumptions.

About this prototype.

Scan4Reuse connects delivery clients and logistics partners. The client view lets you customize and export a delivery request. The partner view demonstrates acceptance for review, itemised quotes and proposed changes; the client can then accept or request a revision. The scanner, marketplace and report-generator screens illustrate planned supporting services. Sample listings and report figures are examples.

Delivery requests are saved on this instance’s server, where its operator can access them. Partner proposals and example agreements stay in this browser session. No request is automatically sent to a carrier, no shipment is booked and no payment is taken. Please use sample details when trying the example.

The upload workspace is shared and has no account system. Do not upload private or sensitive files. No analytics or third-party tracking is used.
