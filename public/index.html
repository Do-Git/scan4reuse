MISSION, VISION & ESG · BUILT INTO THE JOURNEY

Moving things.
Changing mindsets.

We want reuse to be a practical choice for people and businesses. Reclaimed building components are our first use case; the bigger ambition is to keep useful resources circulating.

OUR MISSION

Make reuse simple to choose and practical to deliver.

Connect scanned objects, marketplace buyers and logistics partners, so a useful item can move from being available to being used again.

OUR VISION

A second life becomes the first choice.

A circular economy where materials and objects can be identified, valued, traded and delivered with less friction — starting with the built environment.

Why start with construction materials?

Our focus is on three practical barriers to reuse: manual effort, time and access to information. Background figures for Swiss construction include 17 million tonnes per year and 24% of emissions attributed to construction.

These contextual figures are not independently verified live metrics. Their definitions and primary sources still need confirmation. They explain the initial focus; they are not a claim of impact achieved by Scan4Reuse.

SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

Global goals. Practical logistics.

How our logistics approach supports five UN Sustainable Development Goals, from collection to a material’s next use.

FIVE MINDS. ONE CIRCULAR IDEA.

It started at a hackathon.
It didn’t stop there.

At Startup Weekend Basel, five of us came together around a circular-economy challenge: how can we make giving things a second life feel effortless?

We saw the gap between discovering something reusable and getting it to the person who needs it. Scan4Reuse grew from that idea: connect the scan, the sale and the journey, and make reuse easier to put into practice.

About this prototype.

Scan4Reuse is an early-stage circular logistics platform. The delivery planner lets you prepare, save and export a brief to share with a logistics partner. The scanner, marketplace and report-generator screens illustrate the planned supporting services. Sample listings and report figures are not live transactions or verified project results.

Delivery briefs are saved on this instance’s server. The operator of the instance can access them. No request is automatically sent to a carrier, no shipment is booked and no payment is taken. Please use sample details when trying the demo.

The upload workspace is shared and has no account system. Do not upload private or sensitive files. No analytics or third-party tracking is used.
